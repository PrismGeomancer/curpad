01

Observe

Eight self-report questions and four fictional market scenarios capture your stated habits and decisions. Response times are recorded as context, never used to infer your mental health.

02

Interpret

A deterministic, equal-weight scoring model maps answers to four dimensions. Scores indicate patterns in these answers, not investment skill, medical conditions or future returns.

03

Practice

Get practical reflection tasks matched to your strongest pattern. Reassess to compare answers over time. Checking off a task does not change your score.

Is CURE actually powered by AI?

CURE uses an explainable, rules-based diagnostics engine. No language model is running. A future AI explanation layer could narrate these measured results, with appropriate data controls.

What does CURE keep about me?

Answers, reports, recovery check-ins, decision plans, reviews and practice history stay on this device. Nothing is sent to a CURE server. Export a backup from your patient chart. Clearing browser data removes your history. Google Fonts may receive normal font requests. The hero image and animation libraries are bundled locally.

Can I use my wallet or sync across devices?

This clinic does not connect wallets or read trading history. You can export and import a patient backup manually. Automatic sync and a real community ward would need a separate backend.
